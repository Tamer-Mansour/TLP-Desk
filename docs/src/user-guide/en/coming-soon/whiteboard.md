# Whiteboard

> **Coming soon.** This feature is planned but not available yet. It is part of **M4 — Power features** in the [roadmap](https://github.com/Tamer-Mansour/TLP-Desk/blob/TLP-Desk/ROADMAP.md).

## What it will do

Draw on the remote screen to point things out — useful for support and teaching. Strokes are shown on the host's screen and can be cleared at once.

Needs the **Whiteboard** permission.

## Status

Whiteboard messages are defined in protocol v4.

## Until then

Move the mouse slowly to point; the person at the host sees a green pointer with your name.
