# Sound from the remote PC

> **Coming soon.** This feature is planned but not available yet. It is part of **M2 — Daily-driver features, Lane C** in the [roadmap](https://github.com/Tamer-Mansour/TLP-Desk/blob/TLP-Desk/ROADMAP.md).

## What it will do

Hear the remote PC's sound (system audio) on your PC during the session, with a mute button.

Needs the **Audio** permission.

## Status

Audio control is defined in protocol v4 and the sound will travel on its own high-priority stream (compressed with Opus).

## Until then

None — sound is not transmitted yet.
