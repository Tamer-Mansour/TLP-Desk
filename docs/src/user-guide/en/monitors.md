# Switching monitors

![Monitor buttons in the session toolbar](../../media/monitors-en-light.png)

If the remote PC has **two or more monitors**, the toolbar shows a small monitor icon (its tooltip
says **Screen**) followed by one button per monitor: **1**, **2**, … They are numbered by position, left to right (then top to bottom), the
way the monitors are arranged in the remote PC's display settings.

- Hover over a button to see its number and resolution, and whether it is the **Main screen**.
- Click a button to switch. The picture changes to that monitor within a moment; the button
  highlights once the remote PC has switched.
- Your mouse controls the monitor you are viewing.
- Any key or button you were holding is released when you switch.

The session starts on the remote PC's main monitor. If a monitor is plugged in or removed during
the session, the buttons update by themselves within a few seconds.

With only one monitor, no picker is shown.

## All monitors at once

The last button in the picker (the grid icon, tooltip **All screens**) shows **every monitor in one
picture** of the remote PC's whole desktop, laid out the way the monitors are arranged there
(including monitors placed to the left of or above the main one). The picture is scaled down to the
current quality limit, so text is smaller than on a single monitor. Your mouse still controls the
right spot: a click lands on the monitor under the pointer. Click a number to go back to one
monitor.
