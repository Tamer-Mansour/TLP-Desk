# User guide

This guide explains how to install TLP-Desk and use it to see and control another Windows PC on
your local network.

## What you need

- Two (or more) PCs running **Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit**.
- Both PCs on the **same local network**: the same router, office switch or Wi-Fi. TLP-Desk does
  not work across the internet — by design.
- TLP-Desk installed (or the portable version unzipped) on **both** PCs.

You do not need an account, an internet connection or any server.

## Two roles, one program

| | The PC you sit at | The PC you control |
|---|---|---|
| Called | **viewer** | **host** |
| Shows | the other PC's screen | a banner saying who is connected |
| You use | your mouse and keyboard as usual | nothing — or take over at any time |

Every TLP-Desk can be both. While the program is open, it can receive connections and make them.

## The short version

1. Install TLP-Desk on both PCs and start it.
2. On the PC you want to control, note the **device ID** (9 digits) or find it in the other PC's
   list of **Devices on this network**.
3. On your PC, type the ID (or click the device) and press **Connect**.
4. On the other PC, someone clicks **Accept** — or you type the **one-time code** shown there to
   connect without a prompt.
5. You now see and control the other PC. Click **Disconnect** when you are done.

The pages that follow explain each step and every button.
