# Code of Conduct

TLP-Desk follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: we want a friendly, harassment-free community for everyone, whatever their age, body,
ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance,
religion, language or sexual orientation. Be kind and respectful, accept constructive criticism,
and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal or political
attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

Contributions and discussion in Arabic and in English are equally welcome.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, Tamer Mansour, through GitHub
(<https://github.com/Tamer-Mansour>). All reports are reviewed and kept confidential. The
maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues, and may ban
contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
